Resolution at FSC crossings (Å)

cryoSPARC reports the first crossing (first point dropping below threshold). Last crossing shown for reference. Resolutions are linearly interpolated in frequency (1/Å) between the two shells straddling the threshold, then converted to Å.

Box size and pixel size needed

This file has no frequency column, only wave_number. Give the box size and pixel size of the reconstruction to convert to frequency (1/Å):
frequency = wave_number / (box × pixel size)
